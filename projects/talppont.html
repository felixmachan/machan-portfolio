Massage Parlour Website — Talppont

A static website for Talppont, a massage parlour in Vác, with a focus on a calm, attractive design.

ToolingHTML · CSS · JS
01

Problem

One of my close friends’ mother wanted a simple and calming website for her massage parlour. She knew I was in web development and asked for my help. This is the result.

02

Goal

To design and develop a website that makes it easy to get the most important information about the massage parlour and its services — for example the types of treatments and prices.

03

Approach

The main colors of the brand are royal blue and baby blue, so the design was built around them. The goal was a calming atmosphere through color, typography and imagery, with generous white space to keep the content digestible.
